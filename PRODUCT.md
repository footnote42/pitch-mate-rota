# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Volunteer age-grade rugby coaches at Trojans RFC (U7-U12), managing one squad on a festival day. They plan at home or in the car park before the festival, and adjust pitchside between games, phone in one hand, often in daylight, cold or rain, with children and parents around. Some coaches are confident with the Half Game Rule; many are not.

## Product Purpose

Pitch-Mate-Rota decides who plays which half of which game at a festival, so every child gets fair playing time under the RFU Half Game Rule (Reg 15.13) and each half has a sensible mix of experience. Success: another Trojans coach can pick it up and run a festival unaided, and no child finishes the day short of the minimum without the coach knowing why.

## Positioning

A festival-day tool built around the Half Game Rule and one coach's real pitchside workflow: plan in halves, record what actually happened in quarters, and get honest flags (not blocks) when a choice breaks fairness or balance. Coach judgement always wins.

## Operating Context

- Festival: several short games in one day; age-group player counts and time caps come from RFU Reg 15 (2026-27 season).
- Squad names usually arrive as a WhatsApp squad-selection message, pasted in.
- Early leavers and late arrivals are common; permanent removal (injury, red card) waives the minimum.
- Used mostly on a phone; tablet and desktop for planning ahead.
- Served under `rugby.waynetellis.com/rota`, linked from the rugby hub, installable to work offline.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Plan (halves), Match record (quarters), Auto-fill (fills empty places only, undoable), Squad import, Festival overview, Guide with RFU links.
- Offline-first; no backend, no accounts; no personal data leaves the device. Names are transient: kept on the phone until the coach clears them.
- Terminology: see `CONTEXT.md`.

## Brand Commitments

- Trojans RFC: navy and gold club colours. Audience is Trojans coaches only; no multi-club mode.
- Belongs to the rugby hub (rugby.waynetellis.com, "Chalk & Turf"), sharing its H-posts mark.

## Product Principles

1. The plan is the coach's; the app suggests and flags, it never overrules.
2. Fairness is visible: every player's time against the minimum is always one glance away.
3. Works pitchside: one hand, bad light, no signal.
4. Nothing personal leaves the phone.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Touch targets at least 44x44px. Must stay legible in bright outdoor light and to coaches who are not technical.
