# Early leavers keep the full-day minimum

Reg 15.13(3)(b) (2026-27; was 15.12.3) defines Available Playing Time at a festival as the total of all the team's matches that day, and 15.13(5) (was 15.12.4) exempts only permanent removal for injury, risk of injury or a red card, and abandoned or shortened matches, not leaving early. So an early leaver's minimum is not pro-rated: auto-fill front-loads their games to reach the full minimum, and where that is impossible or needs more than three consecutive halves the plan is flagged rather than silently planned below the regulation. The Half Game Rule outranks the consecutive-halves flag.

## Considered Options

- **Pro-rata minimum (half of the halves they are present for)**: rejected. Kinder to tired players and always achievable, but plans below the regulation without telling the coach.
