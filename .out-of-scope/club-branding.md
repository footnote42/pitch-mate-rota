# Club badge and colour customisation

The rota has one look, Chalk & Turf in Trojans colours, and coaches can't upload a badge or
change the colours.

## Why this is out of scope

The look was decided in the revamp (prototypes in `docs/prototypes/`) and its colours
carry meaning: gold for the one primary action, scarlet for selection, soft orange for
flags. Letting coaches pick colours would break those signals and the contrast checked
in light and dark mode. An uploaded badge needs somewhere to live beyond the single saved
plan, and the app is served under the club's own rugby hub, so it is already club-branded.
