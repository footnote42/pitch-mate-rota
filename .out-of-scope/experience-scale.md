# Finer experience scale

Experience stays at three levels: Novice, Intermediate, Experienced.

## Why this is out of scope

Three levels are enough for the coach to judge each half's mix pitchside, and quick
to set for a whole squad on a phone. Auto-fill's balance target and the "light / heavy on
experience" flags are built on the three-level average, and experience balance is a soft
indicator: coaching judgment, never a block. A 1-5 scale would add precision the coach
doesn't use while making every player slower to rate.
