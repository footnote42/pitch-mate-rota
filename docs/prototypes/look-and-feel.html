Rota: Chalk & Turf with Trojans colours

The same Squad screen in two bases. A keeps the hub's dark frame, with navy in place of turf green. B is chalk on paper, for daylight. Both use the hub's type, dashed chalk lines and H-posts mark, with hooped post pads in club colours, a scarlet chalk line under the header, scarlet for the active tab and early leavers, gold for the one main action. Potential problems are a soft orange, not an alarm. The squad is made up.
