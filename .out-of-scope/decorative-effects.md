# Decorative effects

No decorative animation or ornamental effects: grass-stain selection shapes, scrum-lean
hovers, confetti bursts and the like.

## Why this is out of scope

The rota is a pitchside tool used one-handed on a phone, often in a hurry between games.
The look (Chalk & Turf, from the revamp) puts the character in the type, the chalk rules
and the club colours, and keeps interaction plain:

- Selection is a scarlet state the whole app shares; an organic smudge would blur what is
  picked and what isn't.
- Hover doesn't exist on a phone, and long-press effects fight scrolling and text selection.
- A reward burst needs a new dependency in an offline bundle, and the app already shows
  when every minimum is met (the Half Game Rule status and the share message footer).

Motion that explains a change (a tab switching, a sheet opening) is fine; motion as
decoration is not.
