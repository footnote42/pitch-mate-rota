## Design Context

### Users
Solo coach, phone in one hand on the touchline during a rugby festival. Outdoors, possibly cold, definitely under some pressure. Uses the app pitchside to manage rotations in real time. Needs to read the screen in variable daylight, tap accurately with one thumb, and find information at a glance without stopping to read carefully.

### Brand Personality
Trojans RFC — community, pride, heritage. This is something the club built for itself, not a generic sports product.

Three brand words: **grounded · proud · purposeful**

Emotional goal: a coach opens the app and feels calm authority. Everything they need is right there. The club is behind them.

### Aesthetic Direction
Club-branded, heritage feel. Evokes a matchday programme or the inside of a kit bag — physical, purposeful, worn-in but maintained. Not trendy, not corporate, not "startup sports app."

Light theme. Rugby festivals are daytime outdoor events; light mode reads better in direct sun and on phone screens at arm's length.

**Palette anchors:**
- Deep navy (`oklch(0.30 0.085 245)`) — Trojans RFC primary, dominant surface color in the header and key UI chrome
- Warm amber-gold (`oklch(0.72 0.14 75)`) — club badge accent, used sparingly for highlights and active states
- Warm off-white (`oklch(0.97 0.005 245)`) — slightly navy-tinted background, like the inner pages of a matchday programme
- Tinted neutrals: all grays tinted slightly toward the navy hue for subconscious cohesion

**Typography:**
- Display/headings: **Big Shoulders Display** — condensed, weight-forward, stadium-signage energy. Feels like a coaching board, a kit number, a club fixture list on a notice board.
- UI/body: **Figtree** — clean, warm, readable at small sizes without feeling sterile.

Anti-references: avoid the generic "sports app" look (cyan accents, neon-on-dark, gradient scorecards). Avoid anything that looks like a generic SaaS dashboard. Avoid the current "AI design" monoculture (glassmorphism, gradient text, purple-to-blue).

### Design Principles
1. **Legible at arm's length** — type hierarchy must be so clear that a coach can extract what they need with one glance. Nothing small if it matters.
2. **Club identity first** — the Trojans RFC badge and navy are load-bearing, not decoration. Every design decision should reinforce "this was made for this club."
3. **Touch-first, no exceptions** — every interactive surface must be comfortable to tap with a cold thumb. Minimum 48px touch targets. No hover-only affordances.
4. **Purposeful, not decorative** — details earn their place by reinforcing function. No ornamentation that doesn't serve the coach.
5. **Heritage without nostalgia** — draw on physical/analogue references (matchday programme, coaching board, club notice) for tone, but execute with modern precision.
